Data Understanding workspace analysis

1. ANNUAL INCOME
What we see
Extremely right-skewed distribution
Massive high-income outliers
Default and non-default overlap heavily

The separation is NOT very strong visually.

Banking Interpretation

Higher income alone is NOT enough to determine repayment quality.

This is realistic because:

some high earners still overborrow
debt burden matters more than raw income

VERY IMPORTANT DISCOVERY

Income is heavily skewed.

This is a CLASSIC candidate for:

log transformation

Later we will likely create:

log_annual_income

using:

df['log_annual_income'] = np.log1p(df['annual_income'])

This usually improves:

logistic regression stability
WoE binning
feature separability




2. CREDIT UTILIZATION %

THIS is VERY important.

What we see

Defaulters clearly show:

higher median utilization
wider spread
more concentration at higher values

This is a STRONG risk signal.

Banking Interpretation

Customers using a large percentage of available credit are often:

financially stressed
liquidity constrained
overleveraged

This is one of the BEST credit risk predictors in real banking.

VERY IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

The relationship appears monotonic:

Higher utilization → higher default risk

That is PERFECT for:

logistic regression
WoE binning
scorecards

This is VERY good.



3. DTI RATIO

THIS is another excellent signal.

What we see

Defaulters have:

noticeably higher DTI
higher median
heavier upper tail

Very strong separation.

Banking Interpretation

DTI (Debt-to-Income ratio) measures:

how much debt burden a customer carries relative to income.

Higher DTI means:

less repayment capacity
less disposable income
higher default probability

This is a CORE banking metric.

IMPORTANT OBSERVATION

Again:
relationship appears monotonic.

Higher DTI → higher default probability.

Excellent for scorecard modelling.



4. INTEREST RATE

VERY interesting.

What we see

Defaulters received noticeably higher interest rates.

This suggests:

the lender already risk-priced applicants
higher-risk borrowers were charged more
Banking Interpretation

This is realistic banking behavior.

Interest rates may already contain embedded risk assessment information.

VERY IMPORTANT WARNING

This feature could become VERY predictive.

BUT:

You must later check whether:

it introduces leakage
it is “too close” to the target

Because if rates were assigned using internal risk scoring,
then the model indirectly contains existing model decisions.

This is VERY important.

We will investigate later.


5. NUMBER OF DELINQUENCIES

THIS is one of your BEST predictors so far.

What we see

Defaulters clearly show:

more prior delinquencies
much higher spread
higher median

Very strong separation.

Banking Interpretation

Past bad repayment behavior is one of the strongest predictors of future default.

This is extremely realistic.

IMPORTANT DISCOVERY

This variable is:

discrete
monotonic
interpretable
scorecard friendly

VERY GOOD for WoE.
